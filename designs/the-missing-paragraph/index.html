Closing the darkroom

A note for studio volunteers

The last print

At 8:40 p.m., ring the second bell. It marks the end of new prints and leaves twenty minutes to finish rinsing.

Paragraph removed

After the second bell, finish the print you’re rinsing and switch off the enlargers.

Wet prints

The red rack in the hall is for prints that still need to dry. It stays accessible even after we lock the darkroom.

Paragraph removed

Leave any wet prints on the red rack and write your name on the clip.

Locking up

If someone arrives after the bell, offer them a slot tomorrow. Check the red rack for unlabeled prints before you turn out the lights.
