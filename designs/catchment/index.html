How the model works

Rain falls at a constant rate for 30 minutes on a fictional workshop courtyard. The roof contributes runoff from an effective area of 84 m² and the paving from 54 m².

Each placement gives the garden 900 L of storage. Water soaks into the soil at up to 16 L/min at the roof edge, 10 L/min at the court inlet, and 4 L/min in the low corner. Water that bypasses or overflows the basin goes to the door.

The model uses fixed routes and instant travel. Terrain heights, stream widths, and pooled water are illustrative. The door reading is cumulative volume. Site grading, drains, and water depth would need a separate assessment.
