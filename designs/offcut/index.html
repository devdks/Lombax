    How Offcut works

    1. Enter your stock and pieces. Offcut finds the fewest equal-length boards needed for straight crosscuts. Enter the usable length after squaring the ends, and include the width your blade removes.
    2. Choose the leftovers you can use. Set the shortest length you’d keep. Compare saving one long offcut with saving as many pieces above your minimum as possible.
    3. Take the measurements to your saw. Open Cut marks on any board for positions measured from the same squared end, or print all the boards and marks together.
